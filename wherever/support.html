支援與常見問題

English

需要準備什麼？

iOS 27 以上的 iPhone、開啟「開發者模式」，以及 App Store 上的 LocalDevVPN。第一次打開 App 會帶你完成配對和連線，大約兩分鐘，不需要電腦。

為什麼要關閉行動數據？

開著行動數據時，手機上的開發者通道無法建立。只有「連線那一下」需要關閉，連上後就可以再打開，定位會維持。在「設定 › 行動數據」加入自動切換捷徑後，App 會幫你自動切換。

顯示「未連線 VPN」或「已中斷」

打開 LocalDevVPN 確認已連線，再回到 App 點狀態列或「重新連線」。如果還是不行，請確認連線當下行動數據是關閉的。

有些 App 或遊戲讀不到模擬的位置

iOS 會替模擬的位置加上「由軟體模擬」的標記，部分 App 和遊戲會因此拒絕使用。這是系統行為，App 無法移除。你可以在「設定 › 偵測測試」查看目前的狀態。

怎麼回到真實位置？

在模擬中的卡片按「回到真實位置」，確認後就會結束模擬。也可以用捷徑的「停止模擬定位」。

怎麼取消訂閱？

到 iPhone 的「設定 › Apple 帳號 › 訂閱」，或在 App 的「設定 › 會員 › 管理訂閱」。在免費試用期間取消就不會扣款。

Support & FAQ

What do I need?

An iPhone on iOS 27 or later with Developer Mode on, plus LocalDevVPN from the App Store. The first launch walks you through pairing and connecting in about two minutes, no computer needed.

Why turn off Cellular Data?

The on-device developer tunnel can't open while Cellular Data is on. It only needs to be off at the moment of connecting; turn it back on afterwards and the location stays. Add the auto-switch shortcut in Settings › Cellular Data and the app does it for you.

It says “VPN not connected” or “Disconnected”

Open LocalDevVPN and make sure it's connected, then come back and tap the status pill or Reconnect. If that doesn't help, make sure Cellular Data is off at the moment of connecting.

Some apps or games don't use the simulated location

iOS marks simulated locations as “simulated by software”, and some apps and games reject them. This is system behavior that no app can remove. Check Settings › Detection Test to see the current status.

How do I go back to my real location?

Tap Back to Real Location on the card while simulating and confirm. You can also use the Stop Simulated Location shortcut.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In the iPhone Settings app, go to Apple Account › Subscriptions, or in the app go to Settings › Membership › Manage Subscription. Cancel during the free trial and you won't be charged.
