如何刪除「上次」的帳號

「上次」（LastDone）由 LiTzuHsien 開發。你可以隨時刪除帳號和資料。

在 App 裡刪除（最快）

  1. 打開「上次」，點左上角的「群組」。
  2. 往下捲，點「刪除我的帳號」。
  3. 確認後，用 Apple 或 Google 登入過的帳號要再登入一次，確認是你本人。

如果你是某個群組唯一的建立者，要先把另一位成員設成建立者，才能刪除帳號。

沒辦法打開 App 時

請用登入時的 email 寄信到 sean19950831@gmail.com，主旨寫「刪除帳號」。我們確認是你本人後，會在 30 天內刪除。

會刪除什麼、保留什麼

  • 會刪除：你的帳號、email、稱呼、推播的裝置登記、你自己取的群組名稱、你的地點，並讓你退出所有群組。沒有其他成員的群組（包括裡面的事項、紀錄、照片）會整個刪除。
  • 會保留：你在仍有其他成員的群組裡記過的紀錄和建立的事項，屬於那個群組，會繼續留著，顯示為「前成員」。
  • 刪除後不能復原。

How to delete your LastDone account

LastDone (「上次」) is developed by LiTzuHsien. You can delete your account and data at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open LastDone and tap the Group button at the top left.
  2. Scroll down and tap “Delete my account”.
  3. Confirm. If you signed in with Apple or Google, sign in once more to confirm it’s you.

If you are the only owner of a group, make another member an owner first.

If you can’t open the app

Email sean19950831@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, with the subject “Delete account”. After confirming it’s you, we delete your account within 30 days.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: your account, email, display name, push device registrations, your private group nicknames and your places; you are removed from all groups. Groups with no other members (including their items, records and photos) are deleted entirely.
  • Kept: records and items you created in groups that still have other members belong to that group and remain, shown as “former member”.
  • Deletion cannot be undone.
