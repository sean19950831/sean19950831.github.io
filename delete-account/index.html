如何刪除「上次」的帳號

「上次」（LastDone）由 LiTzuHsien 開發。你可以隨時刪除帳號和資料。

在 App 裡刪除（最快）

  1. 打開「上次」，點左上角的「群組」。
  2. 往下捲，點「刪除我的帳號」。
  3. 確認後，用 Apple 或 Google 登入過的帳號要再登入一次，確認是你本人。

如果你是某個群組唯一的建立者，要先把另一位成員設成建立者，才能刪除帳號。

刪帳號不會取消訂閱。請另外到 App Store 或 Google Play 管理自動續訂，以免繼續扣款。不必等訂閱到期才能刪帳。

沒辦法打開 App 時

請用登入時的 email 寄信到 sean19950831@gmail.com，主旨寫「刪除帳號」。我們確認是你本人後，會在 30 天內刪除。

會刪除什麼、保留什麼

  • 會刪除：你的帳號、email、稱呼、推播的裝置登記、你自己取的群組名稱、你的地點，以及分享對象只有你自己的事項和它們的紀錄、照片、貼紙，並讓你退出所有群組。沒有其他成員的群組（包括裡面的事項、紀錄、照片）會整個刪除。
  • 會保留：和別人共用的事項和紀錄會留著，作者顯示為「前成員」。為了防止重複領獎，可能會保留經雜湊處理的購買識別碼和必要的獎勵紀錄。
  • 刪除後不能復原。

How to delete your LastDone account

LastDone (「上次」) is developed by LiTzuHsien. You can delete your account and data at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open LastDone and tap the Group button at the top left.
  2. Scroll down and tap “Delete my account”.
  3. Confirm. If you signed in with Apple or Google, sign in once more to confirm it’s you.

If you are the only owner of a group, make another member an owner first.

Deleting your account does not cancel subscriptions. Manage auto-renewal separately in the App Store or Google Play to avoid further charges. You do not need to wait for the subscription to expire before deleting your account.

If you can’t open the app

Email sean19950831@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, with the subject “Delete account”. After confirming it’s you, we delete your account within 30 days.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: your account, email, display name, push device registrations, your private group nicknames, places, and items shared only with you together with their records, photos and tags; you are removed from all groups. Groups with no other members (including their items, records and photos) are deleted entirely.
  • Kept: shared items and records stay, with the author shown as “former member”. We may keep hashed purchase identifiers and necessary reward records to prevent duplicate claims.
  • Deletion cannot be undone.
