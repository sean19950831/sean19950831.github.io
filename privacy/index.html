「上次」隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 10 月 13 日

「上次」（英文名稱 LastDone，以下稱本 App）由 LiTzuHsien 開發。本 App 用來記錄生活裡的事上次是什麼時候做的，並和家人、同事一起看、一起提醒。我們只收集讓這些功能運作所需要的資料。

我們收集哪些資料

  • 帳號：每個使用者有一組系統產生的帳號編號。選擇用 Apple 或 Google 登入時，會取得該帳號的 email（Apple 可能是隱藏的轉寄信箱），只用來登入，只顯示在你自己的畫面上，其他成員看不到。
  • 你輸入的內容：群組名稱、你的稱呼、事項（名稱、圖示、顏色、時間設定、分享和提醒的對象）、紀錄（誰在什麼時候做了、選填的數字例如里程）、事項的照片、NFC 貼紙的編號。
  • 裝置資料：推播通知用的裝置代碼、App 版本、語系，用來傳送提醒。
  • 你設定的地點：你存的地點（例如公司、老家的名稱、位置和範圍）和在那裡要先看的事項，用來在你到了附近時，把那些事項放在最上面。只有你看得到。手機現在的位置只在手機上比對，不會上傳。
  • 防濫用：使用 Apple App Attest／Google Play Integrity 確認請求來自正版 App，不會取得你的個人資料。

本 App 沒有廣告、不追蹤你、不使用分析工具，也不會出售你的資料。

資料用在哪裡、誰看得到

  • 資料用來同步你的事項和紀錄、在到期時提醒你和其他成員。
  • 同一個群組裡，被分享的成員看得到那些事項、紀錄、照片和大家的稱呼。沒有分享給他的事項，他看不到。
  • 資料存放在 Google Firebase（位於台灣的 Google Cloud 資料中心）。推播透過 Apple 和 Google 的推播服務傳送。登入由 Apple、Google 提供。
  • 付費訂閱由 App Store／Google Play 處理，我們拿不到你的付款資料。訂閱狀態由 RevenueCat 驗證和保存（購買紀錄和你的帳號編號，不含付款資料），用來讓同一個帳號在 iPhone 和 Android 都能使用。

保存與刪除

  • 帳號存在期間我們會保存你的資料。你可以隨時在 App 裡刪除帳號，方法見如何刪除帳號。
  • 刪除帳號會刪除你的帳號、個人資料、裝置登記，並讓你退出所有群組。你在別人的群組裡記過的紀錄會留著，顯示為「前成員」；沒有其他成員的群組會整個刪除。
  • 登出時，手機上的資料會清除，雲端的資料保留，再登入就會回來。

兒童

本 App 不是專為 13 歲以下兒童設計，我們不會刻意收集兒童的個人資料。

LastDone Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

LastDone (「上次」, “the app”) is developed by LiTzuHsien. It helps you record when things were last done and share them with family or coworkers in groups, with reminders. We only collect what the app needs to work.

What we collect

  • Account: a system-generated user ID. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive that account’s email address (Apple may provide a private relay address). It is used only for sign-in and is shown only to you, never to other members.
  • Content you enter: group names, your display name, items (name, icon, color, schedule, who they are shared with and who gets reminders), records (who did it and when, plus optional numbers such as mileage), item photos, and NFC sticker IDs.
  • Device data: a push notification token, app version and language, used to deliver reminders.
  • Places you set: places you save (for example, the name, location and radius of your office or your parents’ home) and the items you want to see first there, used to show those items at the top when you are nearby. Only you can see them. Your current location is compared on the phone only and is never uploaded.
  • Abuse prevention: Apple App Attest / Google Play Integrity verify that requests come from the genuine app. They do not give us personal data.

The app has no ads, no tracking and no analytics, and we never sell your data.

How it is used and who can see it

  • Your data is used to sync items and records and to remind you and other members when something is due.
  • Members of a group can see the items, records, photos and display names shared with them. Items not shared with someone are hidden from them.
  • Data is stored in Google Firebase (Google Cloud, Taiwan region). Notifications are delivered through Apple and Google push services. Sign-in is provided by Apple and Google.
  • Subscriptions are handled by the App Store / Google Play. We never receive your payment details. Subscription status is verified and stored by RevenueCat (purchase records and your account ID, no payment details) so the same account works on both iPhone and Android.

Retention and deletion

  • We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account in the app at any time; see How to delete your account.
  • Deleting your account removes your account, personal data and device registrations, and removes you from all groups. Records you made in other people’s groups stay and are shown as “former member”. Groups with no remaining members are deleted entirely.
  • Signing out clears the data on the phone. Your data stays in the cloud and returns when you sign in again.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

For privacy questions or help deleting your data, email sean19950831@gmail.com. We will update this page and notify you in the app about significant changes.
