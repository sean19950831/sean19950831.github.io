非禮勿Cam 隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 11 月 18 日・English

一句話：非禮勿Cam 用一支放在家裡的手機當「主機」，連接你的攝影機並錄影；你用另一支手機觀看。影像、錄影和攝影機密碼存在你的手機上，在你的手機之間加密直接傳送。如果你開啟雲端備份，錄影會先在手機上加密，再存到你自己的雲端空間（Google 雲端硬碟、iCloud、OneDrive 或你的 NAS），我們和雲端業者都看不到內容。我們的伺服器協助兩支手機找到彼此、轉送加密的通知，並接收你主動送出的診斷紀錄與相容回報；看不到也不保存你的影像。我們不追蹤你、不放廣告，也不出售任何資料。

本政策說的「手機」也包括 iPad 和 Android 手機：主機、攝影機和觀看端都可以是 iPhone、iPad 或 Android 手機。Android 上的差異另外註明。

本政策說明 非禮勿Cam（以下稱「本 App」，由 非禮勿Cam 開發團隊（以下稱「我們」）提供）如何處理資料。

1. 存在你手機上的資料

以下資料存在你自己的手機上（以及你自己選擇的外接媒體或雲端，見各項說明），我們拿不到：

  • 攝影機的影像、聲音與錄影：存在主機手機上（手機當攝影機使用時，存在那支手機上）。超過你設定的容量時，會自動刪除最舊的錄影；你也可以隨時在 App 裡刪除。主機也可以把錄影寫到你接上的外接記憶卡或隨身碟：在 iPhone／iPad 上，移除 App 不會刪除外接媒體上的錄影；在 Android 上，錄影存在外接媒體上本 App 專屬的資料夾，移除 App 會一併刪除（移除時已拔下的媒體無法清除，請接回並確認錄影已刪除），要保留請先匯出。觀看外出攝影機的即時畫面時若發生動靜，觀看手機會把收到的畫面另存一段影片（最長 10 分鐘）在這支手機上；在 iPhone／iPad 上可能隨系統備份保存，在 Android 上不會進入雲端備份或裝置移轉。若你開啟雲端備份，另有一份加密副本存在你自己的雲端（見第 4 節）。
  • 聲音辨識：主機在本機分析攝影機收音，標記貓叫、狗叫、煙霧警報等聲音事件；你提供的分類回饋與聲音特徵也只存在主機上。刪除回饋後，已標記在錄影上的事件會保留。
  • 攝影機的帳號密碼、小米帳號的登入資訊：存在主機手機的鑰匙圈（Keychain）。開啟設定備份時，攝影機的帳號密碼另有加密副本（見下方「設定備份」）；小米登入資訊不會備份。
  • 配對金鑰：每支手機自己產生金鑰；配對主機時以 QR code 交換公開金鑰。手機攝影機的配對 QR code 或邀請連結含有配對密碼，在有效期間內任何拿到的人都能用它配對，請不要公開分享。
  • 動靜紀錄、縮圖與 App 的記錄檔（錯誤與狀態）：存在手機上，不會進 iCloud 或電腦備份。除非你自己分享或送出（見第 6 節），否則不會傳給任何人；例外是外出攝影機的動靜通知可能附上一張加密縮圖，經我們的伺服器與推播服務轉送，我們解不開（見第 3 節）；已配對並開啟通知的觀看手機也可能經加密直連向外出攝影機取得較清晰的縮圖。你在 App 裡「分享診斷記錄」時，已知的攝影機與主機名稱會換成代號，移除可辨識的 IP／MAC 位址，並遮蔽符合規則的密碼、token 與長十六進位字串；內容包含各攝影機偵測到動靜的時間。
  • 依地點通知：自行新增的地點名稱、座標、半徑及到達／離開規則儲存在觀看手機，不進 iCloud 或電腦備份。非禮勿Cam 不向主機或自有伺服器傳送座標；地點規則由手機的系統定位服務在觀看手機上比對，到達／離開時只向主機或外出攝影機同步暫停／恢復通知的設定。Android 觀看手機的地點通知規則目前保存在手機上、尚未同步到家中主機，但手動捷徑仍會向主機送出暫停／恢復要求，連上外出攝影機時也可能送出依地點算出的暫停期限；座標都不會離開觀看手機。若你也加入居家／外出判斷，兩項功能共用這個家的「家」圓圈，修改圓圈會同時影響兩項功能；刪除圓圈或取消套用到這個家會退出參與。刪除地點可移除該地點設定；刪除不會自動改變目前的通知暫停狀態。在 Android 上，開啟這些功能的觀看手機會以常駐通知在背景定位。
  • 主機上的配對清單與推播金鑰：存在主機手機上，不會進 iCloud 或電腦備份。主控手機可以讀取成員名稱與權限，用於設定備份。
  • 雲端帳號的登入資訊：你在主機上登入 Google 時，登入資訊直接存在主機；在主控手機輸入 NAS 帳密或登入 OneDrive 時，會經加密的配對連線交給家中主機保管；外出攝影機則由主控手機登入 Google 或 OneDrive 後，把憑證交給攝影機保管。登入資訊存在那支手機的鑰匙圈（Android 為系統金鑰庫保護的儲存區），不進 iCloud 或電腦備份，也不會傳給我們。外出攝影機的主控手機會另外保留一份瀏覽用副本；外出攝影機與觀看手機的分享見第 2 節。
  • 雲端錄影的加密金鑰：在主機或外出攝影機上產生，存在那支手機的鑰匙圈，不同步、不進備份。外出攝影機會把金鑰交給已配對的觀看手機（見第 2 節）。
  • 設定備份（自選）：開啟後，主控手機會從主機取得設定快照，包括攝影機清單與連線資料（含帳號密碼；小米攝影機含來源網址中的區域、區網位址、裝置識別碼與型號，但不含小米登入憑證）、成員名稱與權限、路由器位址與指紋，在主控手機上加密並保留數個版本；主機也可以把加密的設定備份上傳到你選的雲端。加密用的復原金鑰由主控手機產生：在 iPhone／iPad 上可透過你的 iCloud 鑰匙圈同步（由 Apple 加密）；在 Android 上不會自動跨裝置同步，也不進系統備份（若你在 Android 主機另行輸入並保存，主機也會保有一份），換手機前請匯出並保存復原碼。持有復原碼的人可以解開設定備份並接手主控，請妥善保管。成員資料還原後仍須重新配對。

2. 在你的手機之間傳送的資料

  • 居家／外出模式（自選）：主控邀請後，只有你在自己手機上同意加入才會啟用。與「依地點通知」共用的「家」圓圈（座標與半徑）只儲存在這支手機，不進 iCloud 或電腦備份。App 只經由與你家主機之間的加密直接連線，傳送「在家／不在家／未確認」、原因與發生時間，不傳送座標。主機據此切換攝影機動作，保存每支參與手機的最新狀態與最近 30 天內、最多 50 筆切換紀錄。我們的伺服器只協助建立連線，不會收到這些狀態。主控手機看得到每支參與手機在不在家；其他家人只看到「居家／外出」。你可隨時退出，不需主控同意；退出立即停止回報，連上主機後，主機會刪除這支手機的在家紀錄。
  • 即時畫面、回放與對講：在你的觀看手機和主機手機之間，以端對端加密的直接連線傳送：控制通道是雙方以配對金鑰互相驗證的 TLS 1.3，影像使用 WebRTC 的 DTLS／SRTP 加密，金鑰與配對身分綁定。在家時走家裡的網路；在外面時，兩支手機透過網路「打洞」直接連線。如果無法建立直接連線，本 App 會顯示連線失敗，不會改由任何伺服器轉送影像（我們不提供影像中繼）。
  • 手機當攝影機（家中攝影機、外出攝影機、視訊通話）：你把手機設為攝影機時，它拍攝的畫面與聲音（收音可關閉）透過加密連線送到你配對的手機，或依你的設定存在本機及你自己的雲端。家中攝影機模式的手機與觀看手機通話時，經你家主機轉接，兩段連線都加密，原本的錄影會繼續。
  • 主機狀態（攝影機是否在線、主機手機的溫度與電量等）：提供給你已配對的觀看手機。
  • 動靜通知的設定：你在觀看手機上打開通知時，它會透過加密的配對連線，把推播代號和一把只屬於這組手機的金鑰交給你的主機手機。金鑰只在你的手機上；推播代號在送出通知時會經過我們的伺服器（見第 3 節）。Android 觀看手機目前尚未開放接收遠端通知。
  • 新配對通知：有新的手機配對到你的主機時，主機會通知已打開通知的主控手機；新手機的名稱與角色用各主控手機的金鑰加密，只有你的手機看得到。
  • 外出攝影機的雲端錄影：為了讓配對過的觀看手機不必另外登入就能播放，外出攝影機會經加密的配對連線，把它的雲端存取憑證與解密金鑰交給每支已配對且連上它的觀看手機，存在觀看手機的鑰匙圈。這份憑證可自動續期，權限與攝影機本身相同：Google 是本 App 在該帳號建立的檔案，OneDrive 是本 App 的資料夾，兩者都可讀寫。觀看手機直接從你的雲端下載並在手機上解密，不經過我們的伺服器。撤銷配對或解除雲端連結後，各觀看手機要在下次連上攝影機時才會刪除這份憑證；已下載的檔案和已取得的金鑰無法遠端收回。要讓憑證失效：Google 請在攝影機上選擇「並停用雲端授權」，攝影機連上 Google 並確認撤銷後才會生效：沒有網路時會排入稍後重試；若連排入重試都失敗，App 會告訴你，授權仍然有效、攝影機可能繼續上傳，請連上網路後再操作一次。生效後，用同一個 Google 帳號登入本 App 的所有裝置（包括家中主機，以及自行登入同一帳號的觀看手機）都需要重新登入；觀看手機自行登入的其他 Google 帳號不受影響。OneDrive 請到 Microsoft 帳戶移除本 App 的權限。解除雲端連結會停止上傳；尚未上傳的事件照片與錄影會加密留在攝影機，之後若連結另一個帳號，會上傳到新帳號。

3. 我們的伺服器會經手的資料

為了讓你在外面也能連回家裡的主機，兩支手機會連到我們的握手伺服器（STUN 與加密的 WebSocket）。伺服器只處理建立直接連線所需的資料：

  • 你的手機的公開 IP 位址與連接埠，用來告訴另一支手機怎麼直接連到你；握手訊息裡也包含手機在家用網路裡的區網位址（例如 192.168.x.x），伺服器轉交時看得到；
  • 由配對金鑰產生的裝置識別碼（用來找到你的主機），以及建立連線用的握手訊息。這些訊息有簽章保護，伺服器無法冒充你的手機，也看不到你的影像或任何內容。
  • 網路狀態檢查：為了判斷在外面能否直接連線，手機與主機會經伺服器交換網路狀態摘要（例如網路位址轉換類型、路由器設定的檢查結果與有效時間），不含影像或其他內容。
  • 動靜通知：主機手機要送通知時，會把觀看手機的推播代號、主機的裝置識別碼，以及用觀看手機金鑰加密過的通知內容交給伺服器，由伺服器轉給 Apple 推播服務；伺服器也會知道通知的種類（例如動靜、主機警示、新配對、確認、測試）、送出的時間，以及用來合併重複通知的識別值。伺服器解不開內容（是哪台攝影機、什麼時間、哪支手機配對），通知上顯示的文字由伺服器固定為「有新的動靜」這類一般文字。外出攝影機的動靜通知可能附上一張用收件手機金鑰加密的小縮圖，同樣只有你的手機能解開。主機的裝置識別碼不會交給 Apple。推播服務的簽署金鑰只放在伺服器上，不在 App 裡；通知內容的加密金鑰只在你的手機上。

訂閱確認（Android，開放 Google Play 訂閱後）：購買時，購買的主控手機把由主機識別碼算出的家用帳號雜湊值交給 Google Play，之後把購買憑證、商品代號與家用帳號雜湊值交給家中主機。主機把這些資料連同主機與這支主控手機的裝置識別碼送到我們的伺服器；伺服器只以購買憑證向 Google Play 查詢。Google 回覆訂閱狀態、到期日、方案、是否為測試購買、供比對的家用帳號雜湊值，以及可能有的先前關聯購買憑證；伺服器把後者雜湊後連同結果做成簽章證明回傳給主機；記錄檔只寫入錯誤代碼與購買憑證的雜湊值。訂閱轉移目前尚未開放；開放前我們會更新本政策，說明伺服器為此保存的資料。

保存方式：上述握手、網路狀態與推播資料只在連線或轉送通知期間存在伺服器的記憶體裡。為了防止濫用，IP 位址會暫存在記憶體中做流量限制，閒置 10 分鐘後刪除；觀看手機與主機的裝置識別碼會在記憶體中做連線請求次數限制，同樣閒置 10 分鐘後刪除；推播代號（以雜湊值保存）與裝置識別碼也會在記憶體中做通知數量限制，閒置一小時後刪除。這些流程不會把 IP 位址、裝置識別碼或推播代號寫入硬碟或記錄檔，唯一寫入硬碟的是「主機曾接受過的觀看手機」清單，讓伺服器重新啟動後，已配對的手機不必和陌生連線請求搶同一份額度：每筆只有一個由「主機＋觀看手機」裝置識別碼算出的雜湊值（SHA-256）和主機最後一次經伺服器回覆連線請求的時間，不含 IP 位址、推播代號或識別碼原文；距主機上次經伺服器回覆該觀看手機的連線請求超過 7 天的不再使用，並在下次清單更新時從硬碟移除，最多保存 10 萬筆。你主動送出的診斷紀錄與相容回報另依第 6 節說明保存。伺服器架設在 Amazon Web Services 東京區域。

4. 第三方服務

  • Apple：動靜通知經由我們的伺服器交給 Apple 推播服務（APNs）傳送。Apple 只看得到一般文字（例如「有新的動靜」）、推播代號和通知送達的資訊；是哪台攝影機、什麼時間，都經過加密，只有你的手機能解開。如果你在 iPhone／iPad 訂閱本 App，付款和訂閱管理都由 Apple 處理，我們拿不到你的付款資訊。訂閱的手機會把 Apple 簽署的訂閱憑證，經加密的配對連線交給它綁定的主機，由主機在本機驗證並保存（不進 iCloud 或電腦備份）；我們的伺服器不經手 Apple 的訂閱或購買資料。
  • Google（Android）：Android 版目前尚未開放遠端動靜通知，通知偏好與地點通知的變更目前只保存在手機上。開放後，Android 的動靜通知會經由 Google 的 Firebase 雲端通訊（FCM）傳送，方式與 Apple 推播相同，我們會先更新本政策。在 Android 上訂閱（開放後）時，付款和訂閱管理由 Google Play 處理，我們拿不到你的付款資訊；訂閱確認見第 3 節。
  • 你的雲端空間（自選）：你開啟雲端備份或外出攝影機的雲端錄影時，手機會直接連到你選的服務。可選的服務依裝置角色與版本而不同，可能包括 Google 雲端硬碟（只能存取本 App 建立的檔案）、iCloud（你自己的 iCloud 私人空間）、Microsoft OneDrive（只限本 App 的應用程式資料夾）或你自己的 NAS（WebDAV）。家中主機上傳錄影片段與索引；外出攝影機上傳事件照片與影片；設定備份需要另外開啟。這些檔案都先在手機上以 AES-256-GCM 加密，金鑰不會交給雲端業者或我們。外出攝影機的資料夾另有一個說明用的純文字檔「請先讀我.txt」。雲端業者看得到檔案數量、大小、上傳時間、隨機檔名、資料夾名稱（外出攝影機的資料夾名稱也經過加密運算），在 iCloud 上還有加密檔的校驗值，看不到影像內容。換雲端時，主機會盡量把加密檔案直接從舊雲端搬到新雲端並逐一驗證；新雲端是無法回報剩餘容量的 WebDAV／NAS，或來源檔損壞時，那些舊錄影會留在舊雲端；新雲端尚未準備好時，新錄影可能暫時繼續存在舊雲端。所有主控手機自動確認收到變更（主控 App 連上主機時會自動回覆）、保護期滿（預設至少 7 天）且新雲端驗證完成後，才會刪除舊雲端的副本；你可以關閉自動清除。這些服務依各自的隱私權政策處理你的帳號與檔案；我們的伺服器不經手雲端檔案或登入資訊。
  • 公用 STUN 服務：在家用網路觀看及部分連線檢查時，App 也會向 Cloudflare 與 Google 的公用 STUN 伺服器查詢，它們會收到你的公開 IP 位址、連接埠與查詢時間。

非禮勿Cam Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

In short: 非禮勿Cam turns a phone at home into a "host" that connects to your cameras and records; you watch from another phone. Video, recordings and camera passwords stay on your phones and travel directly between them, encrypted. If you turn on cloud backup, recordings are encrypted on the phone first and then stored in your own cloud storage (Google Drive, iCloud, OneDrive or your NAS); neither we nor the cloud provider can see the content. Our server helps your phones find each other, forwards encrypted notifications, and receives diagnostic and compatibility reports you choose to send; it cannot see or store your video. We don't track you, show ads, or sell any data.

"Phone" in this policy includes iPad and Android phones: the host, cameras and viewers can each be an iPhone, iPad or Android phone. Differences on Android are noted.

This policy explains how 非禮勿Cam (the "App", provided by the Feiliwucam Team, "we") handles data.

1. Data kept on your phones

The following data is kept on your own phones (and on external media or cloud storage you choose, as noted), and we can't access it:

  • Camera video, audio and recordings are stored on the host phone (or, when a phone is used as a camera, on that phone). The oldest recordings are deleted automatically when they exceed the space you set, and you can delete them in the App at any time. The host can also write recordings to a memory card or USB drive you attach: on iPhone and iPad, removing the App does not delete recordings on external media; on Android, recordings are kept in this App's own folder on the external media and are deleted with the App (media unplugged at that time can't be cleared; reconnect it and check the recordings are gone), so export them first if you want to keep them. When motion happens while you watch an outing camera live, the viewing phone saves a copy of the received picture as a video (up to 10 minutes) on that phone; on iPhone and iPad it may be included in the system backup, while on Android it is excluded from cloud backup and device transfer. If you turn on cloud backup, an encrypted copy is also kept in your own cloud storage (see section 4).
  • Sound recognition: the host analyzes camera audio on the device to mark sound events such as cat meows, dog barks and smoke alarms; your classification feedback and sound features are kept only on the host. Deleting feedback keeps events already marked on recordings.
  • Camera usernames and passwords, and your Xiaomi sign-in are kept in the host phone's Keychain. With settings backup on, camera usernames and passwords also have an encrypted copy (see "Settings backup" below); your Xiaomi sign-in is never backed up.
  • Pairing keys are created on each phone; pairing with a host exchanges public keys by QR code. A phone camera's pairing QR code or invite link contains a pairing secret: while it is valid, anyone who has it can pair, so don't share it publicly.
  • Motion history, thumbnails and app logs (errors and status) stay on the phone, out of iCloud and computer backups, unless you share or submit them yourself (see section 6). The exception: an outing camera's motion notification may include an encrypted thumbnail passed through our server and the push service, which we can't open (see section 3); a paired viewing phone with notifications on may also fetch a clearer thumbnail from the outing camera over an encrypted direct connection. When you use "Share diagnostics" in the App, known camera and host names are replaced with codes, recognizable IP/MAC addresses are removed, and passwords, tokens and long hexadecimal strings matching the redaction rules are masked; the report does include when each camera detected motion.
  • Location-based notifications: place names, coordinates, radii and arrival/departure rules are stored on the viewing phone, outside iCloud and computer backups. 非禮勿Cam does not send coordinates to the host or its own servers. The phone's system location service evaluates place rules on the viewing phone; arrivals and departures sync only the notification pause/resume setting to the host or outing camera . On Android viewing phones, place rules for the home host are currently kept on the phone and not yet synced, but manual shortcuts still send pause/resume requests to the host, and a connected outing camera may receive a pause period computed from your places; coordinates never leave the viewing phone. If you also join Home/Away, both features share this home's Home circle; editing it affects both features. Deleting the circle or removing its assignment to this home ends participation. Deleting a place removes that place setting without changing the current notification pause state. On Android, a viewing phone with these features on uses location in the background with an ongoing notification.
  • The host's pairing list and notification keys stay on the host phone, out of iCloud and computer backups. Controlling phones can read member names and permissions for settings backup.
  • Cloud sign-ins: when you sign in to Google on the host, the credentials are stored directly on the host; NAS credentials or a OneDrive sign-in entered on a controlling phone are handed to the home host over the encrypted pairing link; for an outing camera, the controlling phone signs in to Google or OneDrive and hands the credential to the camera. They are kept in that phone's Keychain (on Android, storage protected by the system keystore), out of iCloud and computer backups, and are never sent to us. An outing camera's controlling phone also keeps a copy for browsing; sharing between an outing camera and viewing phones is described in section 2.
  • Cloud recording encryption keys are created on the host or outing camera and kept in that phone's Keychain, not synced and not backed up. An outing camera hands its key to its paired viewing phones (see section 2).
  • Settings backup (optional): when turned on, the controlling phone gets a settings snapshot from the host, including the camera list and connection details (with usernames and passwords; for Xiaomi cameras the source address with region, local address, device ID and model, but never the Xiaomi sign-in credentials), member names and permissions, and router address and fingerprint, encrypts it on the controlling phone and keeps several versions; the host can also upload an encrypted settings backup to the cloud you choose. The recovery key used for this encryption is created by the controlling phone: on iPhone and iPad it can sync through your iCloud Keychain (encrypted by Apple); on Android it is not synced across devices or included in system backups (if you also enter and save it on an Android host, the host keeps a copy), so export and keep the recovery code before changing phones. Anyone with the recovery code can open the settings backup and take over as controller, so keep it safe. Restored members still need to pair again.

2. Data sent between your phones

  • Home/Away (optional): it starts only after the controller invites you and you agree on your own phone. The Home circle (coordinates and radius), shared with location-based notifications, is stored only on this phone, outside iCloud and computer backups. Over the encrypted direct connection with your home's host, the App sends only "home / away / unknown", the reason and the time, never coordinates. The host uses this to switch camera actions and keeps each participating phone's latest status and up to 50 changes from the last 30 days. Our server only helps set up the connection and never receives these statuses. Controlling phones can see whether each participating phone is home; other family members see only "Home/Away". You can leave at any time without the controller's approval; reporting stops immediately, and the host deletes this phone's home records the next time it connects.
  • Live view, playback and talk travel directly between your viewing phone and the host phone with end-to-end encryption: the control channel is TLS 1.3 with both sides authenticated by their pairing keys, and video uses WebRTC's DTLS/SRTP encryption with keys bound to the paired identities. At home this uses your home network; away from home the two phones connect directly by "hole punching". If no direct connection can be made, the App shows a connection failure; it never falls back to relaying video through any server (we do not offer a video relay).
  • A phone used as a camera (home camera, outing camera, video calls): the picture and sound it captures (sound pickup can be turned off) go over encrypted connections to the phones you paired, or are stored on that phone and in your own cloud as you set it up. A call between a home-camera phone and a viewing phone is bridged by your own host, with both legs encrypted, and the recording continues.
  • Host status (whether cameras are online, the host phone's temperature and battery, and so on) is provided to your paired viewing phones.
  • Notification setup: when you turn on notifications on a viewing phone, it gives your host phone its push token and a key unique to this pair of phones, over the encrypted pairing link. The key stays on your phones; the push token passes through our server when a notification is sent (see section 3). Android viewing phones can't receive remote notifications yet.
  • New pairing alerts: when another phone pairs with your host, the host tells the controlling phones that have notifications on; the new phone's name and role are encrypted with each controlling phone's key, so only your phones can read them.
  • Outing-camera cloud recordings: so that paired viewing phones can play them without signing in, an outing camera hands its cloud access credential and decryption key over the encrypted pairing link to each paired viewing phone that connects to it, where they are kept in the Keychain. The credential renews itself and has the same access as the camera: on Google, the files this App created in that account; on OneDrive, this App's folder; both read and write. Viewing phones download directly from your cloud and decrypt on the phone; nothing passes through our server. After you revoke a pairing or unlink the cloud, each viewing phone deletes the credential the next time it connects to the camera; files already downloaded and keys already received cannot be recalled remotely. To invalidate credentials: on Google, choose "and turn off cloud access" on the camera; it takes effect once the camera reaches Google and the revocation is confirmed. When offline it is queued to retry later; if even queuing fails, the App tells you, the access stays valid and the camera may keep uploading, so try again once online. After it takes effect, every device using this App with that Google account (including your home host and viewing phones that signed in to the same account themselves) must sign in again; other Google accounts a viewing phone signed in to are not affected. On OneDrive, remove this App's access in your Microsoft account. Unlinking the cloud stops uploads; event photos and recordings not yet uploaded stay encrypted on the camera and are uploaded to the new account if you later link a different one.

3. What our server handles

So you can reach your home host from outside, both phones connect to our handshake server (STUN and an encrypted WebSocket). The server handles only what's needed to set up a direct connection:

  • your phone's public IP address and port, so the other phone knows how to reach you directly; the handshake messages also contain the phone's address on your home network (such as 192.168.x.x), which the server can see while passing them on;
  • a device identifier derived from the pairing key (to find your host) and the handshake messages. These messages are signed, so the server can't impersonate your phones, and it can't see your video or any content.
  • Network checks: to tell whether a direct connection is possible from outside, the phones and the host exchange a network-status summary through the server (for example the address-translation type, router-setup check results and how long they are valid). It contains no video or other content.
  • Motion notifications: to send a notification, the host phone gives the server the viewing phone's push token, the host's device identifier and the notification content encrypted with the viewing phone's key; the server passes it to the Apple Push Notification service. The server also knows the notification type (such as motion, host alert, new pairing, confirmation or test), when it was sent, and an identifier used to merge duplicate notifications. It can't read the content (which camera, when, which phone paired), and the text shown is fixed by the server to something generic like "new motion". An outing camera's motion notification may include a small thumbnail encrypted with the receiving phone's key, which likewise only your phone can open. The host's device identifier is never given to Apple. The push service signing key is kept only on the server, not in the App; the keys that encrypt notification content stay on your phones.

Subscription checks (Android, once Google Play subscriptions open): at purchase, the buying controlling phone gives Google Play a home-account hash derived from the host identifier, then hands the purchase token, product code and home-account hash to your home host. The host sends these, with the host's and that controlling phone's device identifiers, to our server; the server queries Google Play with the purchase token only. Google replies with the subscription status, expiry, plan, whether it is a test purchase, the home-account hash for matching, and any previously linked purchase token; the server hashes the latter and returns a signed proof with the result to the host; logs record only error codes and a hash of the purchase token. Subscription transfer is not available yet; before it opens we will update this policy to describe what the server keeps for it.

Retention: the handshake, network-check and notification data above exists only in the server's memory while you connect or a notification is passed on. To prevent abuse, IP addresses are held in memory for rate limiting and forgotten after 10 minutes of inactivity; the viewing phone's and host's device identifiers are held in memory to limit connection requests and are likewise forgotten after 10 minutes of inactivity; push tokens (as hashes) and device identifiers are held in memory to limit notifications and are forgotten after an hour of inactivity. These flows never write IP addresses, device identifiers or push tokens to disk or logs. The only thing written to disk is a list of "viewing phones this host has accepted", so already-paired phones don't compete with unknown connection requests for the same allowance after the server restarts: each entry is only a hash (SHA-256) computed from the host's and viewing phone's device identifiers plus the time the host last answered a connection request through the server, with no IP address, push token or raw identifier. Entries whose host has not answered that viewing phone's connection request through the server for 7 days are no longer used and are removed from disk the next time the list is updated; at most 100,000 entries are kept. Diagnostic reports and compatibility reports you choose to send are kept as described in section 6. The server runs in the Amazon Web Services Tokyo region.

4. Third-party services

Apple sees only generic text (such as "new motion"), the push token and delivery information; which camera and when are encrypted so that only your phone can read them. If you subscribe on an iPhone or iPad, Apple handles payment and subscriptions; we never receive your payment details. The subscribing phone hands Apple's signed subscription record to the host it is bound to, over the encrypted pairing link, and the host checks and keeps it (out of iCloud and computer backups); our server does not handle Apple subscription or purchase data.
  • Google (Android): remote motion notifications are not yet available on Android; notification preferences and place-notification changes are kept only on the phone for now. When they open, Android notifications will be delivered through Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) in the same way as Apple push, and we will update this policy first. If you subscribe on Android (once available), Google Play handles payment and subscriptions and we never receive your payment details; subscription checks are described in section 3.
  • Your cloud storage (optional): when you turn on cloud backup or an outing camera's cloud recording, the phone connects directly to the service you choose. Which services are offered depends on the device's role and the App version, and may include Google Drive (access only to files this App created), iCloud (your own private iCloud storage), Microsoft OneDrive (only this App's app folder) or your own NAS (WebDAV). The home host uploads recording segments and indexes; outing cameras upload event photos and videos; settings backup must be turned on separately. All of these are encrypted on the phone with AES-256-GCM, and the keys are never given to the cloud provider or to us. Outing-camera folders also contain one plain-text note, "請先讀我.txt". The provider can see the number, sizes and upload times of files, their random names and folder names (outing-camera folder names are also derived with a keyed hash), and on iCloud a checksum of each encrypted file, but not the content. When you switch clouds, the host copies the encrypted files directly from the old cloud to the new one where possible and verifies each; if the new cloud is a WebDAV/NAS that can't report free space, or a source file is damaged, those older recordings stay in the old cloud; if the new cloud isn't ready yet, new recordings may stay in the old cloud for a while. Old copies are deleted only after all controlling phones automatically acknowledge the change (the controlling App replies when it connects to the host), the protection period ends (at least 7 days by default) and the new cloud is verified; you can turn automatic clean-up off. Each service handles your account and files under its own privacy policy; our server never handles cloud files or sign-ins.
  • Public STUN services: for viewing on your home network and some connection checks, the App also queries Cloudflare's and Google's public STUN servers, which receive your public IP address, port and the time of the query.
  • Apple Maps: the notification place picker on iPhone and iPad uses Apple MapKit map services. Displaying the map requests map data for the visible map area from Apple, under Apple's privacy policy. This does not mean all geographical information stays on the phone; 非禮勿Cam does not send coordinates to the host or its own servers.
  • OpenStreetMap (Android): the place picker on Android downloads map tiles from OpenStreetMap, which receives your IP address, the map area you view, and the App version and platform.
  • Xiaomi (only if you sign in to a Xiaomi account): the host phone signs in directly with Xiaomi's servers to get your camera list and connection details; Xiaomi's privacy policy applies. Your Xiaomi account details are never sent to us.
  • Your cameras: the App connects to your cameras as you set them up (for example RTSP or ONVIF). Some cameras contact their manufacturer's services on their own; that is governed by the camera and its manufacturer.
  • The App uses no analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

  • When you turn off notifications, the viewing phone asks the host to delete its push token the next time they connect.
  • When you remove a host on a viewing phone, it asks the host to delete that phone's pairing and push token too; if the host can't be reached then, remove the phone on the host.
  • Deleting the App deletes its recordings and settings on that phone (on Android, including recordings on external media; see section 1), but not recordings on an iPhone or iPad's external media, copies on other phones, files in your cloud or videos you exported; handle those separately. iOS may keep Keychain passwords after the App is deleted; to remove them too, remove your cameras, sign out of Xiaomi and remove linked cloud accounts (Google, NAS, OneDrive and so on) in the App first.

Pausing uploads or unlinking an outing camera's cloud does not delete cloud files. Turning off the home cloud backup takes effect 7 days after all controlling phones automatically acknowledge it (the controlling App replies when it connects to the host), and then removes the recordings in the cloud, including recordings that no longer exist on the host and survive only in the cloud; you can cancel on the host before it takes effect. Removing the App under "Third-party apps with account access" in your Google Account requires every device that uses the App with that Google account to sign in again.
  • Handshake and push relay data expires from memory as described in section 3; for diagnostic and compatibility reports you sent, you can request early deletion as described below.

Voluntary camera compatibility reports

非禮勿Cam offers voluntary camera compatibility reports to improve support for different models. A controlling phone, or an iPhone/iPad host that already has paired viewing phones, can send one; family members' phones can only preview. On Android you can also use "Share" to hand the selected JSON and summary to another app you choose, which handles it under its own rules. Declining, cancelling or dismissing the follow-up prompt does not affect your cameras or the App.

Before sending, you can read an item-by-item summary in Chinese, expand the full JSON and untick fields you don't want to send; the report is uploaded to our server only after you tap "Agree and send". Sending requires a verified pairing identity and the host must be online.

A report can include the camera brand, recognized public model, firmware and hardware versions matching known formats, compatibility recipe and verification level, how the camera was added and the result, classified error areas and codes, stream specifications and path templates without addresses, feature results and their basis, and offline tests and test answers you did yourself that the App recorded. Data not obtained is omitted or marked unknown/untested. App and system versions, phone class, and the country/region set in system settings can be included at your choice; no city or more detailed location is included. Reports never include camera or host IP, MAC, serial numbers, usernames or passwords, camera names, home names, Wi-Fi names, push tokens, screenshots, video or sound; raw device strings of unknown models are not sent.

The server keeps the report, a content hash used to detect duplicates, the time, and device and approving-host identifier hashes used for rate limiting for up to 30 days, then deletes them automatically. Once the server confirms receipt, the App shows a receipt number, which you can give us to request early deletion. We use reports only to improve compatibility and handle your related questions, never for advertising or tracking. The phone keeps your report draft (including the JSON you chose) and its receipt status, which you can delete on iPhone/iPad under "Report history" → "Saved reports and drafts", and on Android under "Reports you shared" → "Saved reports" by swiping a draft; deleting only the sharing history does not delete drafts, and deleting on the phone does not delete a report already sent.

Diagnostic reports you actively submit

非禮勿Cam's "Send to developer" uploads only after you preview and confirm. The content matches the redacted export: errors, status, performance records and motion times, App/core/system versions, device model, time zone and this phone's role, with known camera and host names replaced by codes (to do this the App looks up known names on the phone), recognizable IP/MAC addresses removed, and passwords, tokens and long hexadecimal strings matching the redaction rules masked; video, configuration files, key files and pairing credentials are never attached. You can add a description of up to 500 Unicode scalars; the same redaction applies, but rules can't recognize all free text, so check the preview and don't enter personal data or secrets. An outing camera has no upload identity of its own, so after you confirm, its report is uploaded by a paired controlling phone. This is different from sharing through the system share sheet yourself.

We use reports only to troubleshoot and improve 非禮勿Cam; reports are classified as diagnostic or as a reserved bug category (for future bug-report review; no reward is promised). The server verifies the device signature and the host's pairing approval, and keeps the diagnostic record, description, random report number, time, and device and approving-host identifier hashes used for rate limiting for up to 30 days, then deletes them automatically (cleaned every minute while the service runs, or at restart if it was stopped). Only local server administrators can read them; there is no public read API. You can ask the developer for early deletion with the report number; deleting the App does not delete reports already sent.
